Lilly Günther (*in Herzgebirge) is a tattoo artist and illustrator based in Bremen. Her work is deeply rooted in drawing and capturing daily life, which serves as the continuous foundation for her ideas.

She creates a welcoming safe space where you can feel entirely at ease while getting tattooed. Her signature style revolves around unique freehand tattoos, crafted directly on your skin on the day of your appointment. Alongside her custom work, she regularly draws fresh flashes derived from her ongoing illustration practice.
